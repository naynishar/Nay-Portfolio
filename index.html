About Me

The story so far

I'm not your typical PM candidate. I didn't follow the conventional path — I built my own, brick by brick, across two continents and multiple industries.

Born in India, now based in Dublin, I speak four languages and navigate cultures as easily as I navigate stakeholder meetings. My journey started with a simple idea: taking people into the mountains. That idea became MDH Campers, a profitable trekking venture that taught me more about operations, risk management, and customer experience than any classroom could.

From there, I moved into high-velocity sales at Osprey Invites, where I mastered digital lead generation and B2B/B2C conversion. Then to DPD, where I handled 70+ customer queries daily via Salesforce, learning the discipline of logistics and operational excellence.

Today, I'm channeling all of that into my MSc at UCD Smurfit, where my dissertation explores how AI can revolutionize project planning. I'm not just studying the future — I'm building it.
